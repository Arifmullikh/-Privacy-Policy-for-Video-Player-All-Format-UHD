Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Video Player All Format (the "App"), developed by AM Core Studios ("we", "us", "our"), handles your information. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The App has no user accounts and no servers. The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location.

2. Your Media Files

The App needs permission to access the video and audio files on your device so it can play them. All files are processed locally on your device only. Your videos, music, playlists, playback history, settings, Private Folder contents, and custom theme images are never uploaded to any server.

3. Private Folder

Files you place in the Private Folder are stored and protected locally on your device. We cannot access them. If you uninstall the App or forget your lock, these files may not be recoverable.

4. Internet Usage and Payments

The App works fully offline. It uses the internet only to process Premium purchases through Google Play Billing. Payments are handled entirely by Google; we never see or store your payment details. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for details.

5. Advertising

The App contains no ads and uses no advertising or tracking SDKs.

6. Permissions We Use

  • Read media / storage (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED, and READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and below): to find and play the videos and music on your device. On Android 14+, you may allow access to selected videos only.
  • Write storage (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 9 and below only): to save converted MP3 files and rename or delete files you choose.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show playback controls in the notification.
  • Foreground service and wake lock (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK, WAKE_LOCK): to keep music and video playing in the background and with the screen off.
  • Biometric (USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT): optional fingerprint or face unlock for the Private Folder. Biometric data is handled by Android and is never accessed or stored by the App.
  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) and Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): used only to process Premium purchases. No streaming, ads, or other online features.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, phone, all-files storage (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE), or advertising ID.

6a. Third-Party Services

The App contains no analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs. The only third-party service is the Google Play Billing Library. When you buy Premium, the App receives only the purchase status and a purchase token from Google Play, which are stored locally on your device to unlock Premium features.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
