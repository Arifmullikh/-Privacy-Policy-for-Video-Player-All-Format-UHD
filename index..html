THE DETAILS

Privacy, section by section.

Everything you need to know about the data, permissions, and services behind the app.

1. Overview

This policy applies to Video Player All Format UHD (the “App”), developed by AM Core Studios (“we”, “us”, or “our”). It describes the information the App uses and the choices available to you.

The App has no user accounts or company-operated servers. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location, and we do not collect personal information through the App.

2. Your media and app data

The App accesses media you make available on your device so it can find and play videos and audio. Media is processed locally. Your media files, playlists, playback history, bookmarks, favourites, settings, Privacy folder contents, and custom theme images are not uploaded by the App to our servers.

Home screen widgets may display the current track, playback controls, shortcuts, and thumbnails of recently played media. This information is read on the device and appears in widgets you place on your home screen.

3. Privacy folder

If you use the optional Privacy folder to hide videos or protect folders, the App stores those files in the location you choose through Android’s system folder picker. The files are encrypted on your device using AES-256. You can restore them after reinstalling the App if you retain access to the chosen folder and your recovery credentials.

  • Access is protected by a PIN, with optional fingerprint or face unlock. The PIN is not stored in plain text; a salted one-way hash is kept on the device.
  • You may configure a security question for recovery. Your answer is used on the device to derive a recovery key and is not sent to us.
  • We cannot view or recover your protected files, PIN, or security answer. If you lose both your PIN and recovery answer, or delete the encrypted folder, the files may be unrecoverable.
  • When hiding a video, Android may ask you to approve removing its original from the gallery. The App moves it into the encrypted Privacy folder.

Biometric checks are performed by Android. The App does not access or store your biometric data.

5. Permissions

The App requests permissions only for the features described below. Android may show different permission names or prompts depending on your version.

  • Media and storage (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED; READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and below): find and play media you choose. On Android 14 and later, you can grant access to selected videos only.
  • Write storage (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 9 and below): save converted MP3 files, restore protected videos, and rename or delete files when you request it.
  • Folder access you grant through Android’s system picker: save encrypted Privacy folder files in the location you select.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): show playback controls in notifications.
  • Foreground service and wake lock (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK, WAKE_LOCK): continue playback in the background or with the screen off.
  • Biometric (USE_BIOMETRIC): optional device-managed fingerprint or face unlock for the Privacy folder.
  • Internet, network state, and Google Play Billing (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, com.android.vending.BILLING): policy access and Premium purchases.

The App does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, phone, all-files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE), or advertising ID.

6. Third-party services

The App includes no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. Google Play Billing is the third-party service used for purchases. The App also includes open-source Inter, Poppins, and Roboto fonts under the SIL Open Font License; the fonts do not collect data.

7. Retention and deletion

App data is stored on your device. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes app settings, history, and playlists. Encrypted Privacy folder files remain in the location you selected until you restore them, delete them, or remove that folder.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the App or its practices change. We will post the revised policy here and update the “Last updated” date.
